# ACI Infotech overview

ACI Infotech describes itself as an enterprise engineering firm focused on building
modern data foundations, adding AI capabilities, and operating the resulting systems in
production. Its main capability areas include data engineering and lakehouse platforms,
applied AI and generative AI, cloud modernization, and managed operations with
site-reliability engineering.

The company says it serves financial services, healthcare, retail and consumer,
hospitality, manufacturing, energy and utilities, and transportation. Its typical work is
aimed at large enterprises that require regulated, production-grade systems.

The technology ecosystem listed by ACI includes Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft Azure,
AWS, Google Cloud, SAP, ServiceNow, Salesforce, and Braze. AI delivery may also involve
model providers and orchestration tools such as Anthropic, OpenAI, and LangGraph.

ACI's website reports that the company was founded in 2006 and has more than 1,200
engineers, more than 500 enterprise projects, and 11 global delivery hubs. It also lists
ISO 27001:2022 certification and CMMI Level 3.

The website presents ArqAI Labs as a forward-deployed AI engineering practice and ACI
Interactive as a specialist division for marketing technology, customer-data platforms,
and journey orchestration.
