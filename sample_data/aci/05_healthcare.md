# Healthcare and Life Sciences

ACI's healthcare offering focuses on compliant data platforms, clinical interoperability,
claims analytics, population-health analytics, and governed AI for providers, payers, and
life-sciences organizations.

The page describes integrating EHR, claims, laboratory, device, and operational data into
a governed patient view. Interoperability work uses FHIR APIs and HL7 feeds, with controls
designed around HIPAA and HITRUST expectations.

Population-health capabilities include risk stratification, care-gap identification, and
outcome prediction using current data. Research and life-sciences work includes unified
research platforms, automated lineage, and controlled access to data used by discovery
teams.

For revenue-cycle operations, ACI discusses automated claims analysis, denial prediction,
payment optimization, and document AI. The page reports an example in which claims
processing was reduced from 72 hours to four hours with 88% automated accuracy.

Healthcare solutions are described in terms of operational measures such as HEDIS scores,
readmission rates, denial rates, and days in accounts receivable. Relevant technologies
listed on the page include Databricks, MLflow, Power BI, Kafka, Snowflake, Python, AWS,
FHIR, and HL7.

The page also outlines managed-services engagements covering ongoing platform operation,
monitoring, and compliance reporting for healthcare clients after initial delivery.
