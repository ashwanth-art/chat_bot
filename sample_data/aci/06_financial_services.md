# Financial Services

ACI's financial-services practice addresses infrastructure modernization, AI adoption,
regulatory requirements, security, and operational reliability for organizations ranging
from regional banks to global asset managers.

The page describes data-platform modernization as consolidation of legacy systems into
cloud-native foundations that support real-time analytics and AI while avoiding reporting
disruption. Governance capabilities include data lineage, audit trails, and control
frameworks intended to support requirements such as SOX, SOC 2, and PCI-DSS.

AI use cases include real-time fraud detection with reduced false positives, risk scoring,
scenario modelling, and personalization. Customer-360 work unifies data across customer
touchpoints to support more relevant engagement and cross-selling.

The financial-services page reports example outcomes including more than 40 systems
consolidated, over $12 million in annual savings, no reporting disruption, $25 million in
fraud reduction, 85% fewer false positives, and fraud scoring below 100 milliseconds.
These figures are presented by ACI as project outcomes and should be treated as company
claims rather than independent benchmarks.

ACI links these solutions to its data engineering, cloud modernization, applied AI and ML,
cybersecurity, and digital-transformation capabilities.
