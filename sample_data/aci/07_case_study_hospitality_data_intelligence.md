# Global food and facilities leader: unified data intelligence

ACI describes a data-intelligence transformation for a Fortune 500 hospitality and
facilities-management client operating across 34 countries. The engagement addressed
fragmented operational and procurement data, slow reporting, regional data definitions,
manual reconciliation, and delayed retail updates.

ACI used a hub-and-spoke architecture with Databricks as a central processing engine,
Snowflake as the enterprise warehouse, and AWS infrastructure. Four parallel workstreams
covered global spend intelligence, migration of the DRIVE food-management platform to
Microsoft Dynamics 365 with Informatica IICS, real-time retail data using AWS Kinesis,
and integrations between NexTep, Retail Ranger, and the central data hub.

The official case study reports a reduction in implementation time from 36 months to 12
months, described as a 67% reduction. It also reports $4.7 million in annual procurement
savings, 15-times faster decision-making, and elimination of manual reconciliation.
Other page copy reports 78% faster data processing, $2.3 million in annual cost savings,
65% lower data redundancy, and a 23% improvement in customer satisfaction. These are
ACI-published project claims and should be attributed as such rather than treated as
independent benchmarks.

Technologies named in the case study include Informatica IICS, Informatica IDMC,
Databricks, Snowflake, AWS, AWS Kinesis, Microsoft Dynamics 365, NexTep, and Retail
Ranger.
