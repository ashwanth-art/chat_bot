# Finance and reporting modernization with SAP

ACI describes a transformation for a global investment-analytics organization whose
finance teams relied on SAP ECC, SAP BPC, and SAP BW. The legacy environment contributed
to a ten-day month-end close, long allocation runs, manual reconciliation, limited
segregation of duties, and slow reporting changes.

ACI used a phased migration from SAP ECC to SAP HANA while maintaining business
continuity. The work upgraded BPC and BW, rewrote more than 72 allocation modules using
optimized ABAP, connected ECC, BW, and BPC through streamlined ETL, added automated error
recovery, implemented role-based access controls, and produced SOX-oriented audit trails
and data lineage.

ACI reports a 67% reduction in allocation processing time, $500,000 in annual operational
savings, 8.5-times faster query performance, and a zero-downtime migration. The detailed
results also describe 50% faster allocation runs, a month-end close reduced from ten days
to seven days, zero SOX audit findings, and 100% uptime during migration. These figures
are ACI-published project outcomes.
