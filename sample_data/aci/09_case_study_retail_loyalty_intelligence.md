# Loyalty with real-time customer intelligence

The supplied ACI case study describes a convenience-retail and fuel-distribution client
with more than 750 stores. Loyalty transactions, mobile activity, promotions, and partner
data were fragmented across systems, which limited personalization, mobile-performance
analysis, partner reconciliation, forecasting, and measurement of loyalty-program return
on investment.

ACI built a Loyalty Data Processing Engine on Databricks to unify point-of-sale, mobile,
web, partner API, and third-party data under Unity Catalog. The solution added real-time
mobile analytics, automated partner data sharing and deficit-points calculation,
ML-based participation forecasting, revenue-walk analysis, omnichannel workflows, and a
fraud-detection dashboard.

The document reports a 40% increase in program engagement, complete visibility into
mobile analytics, $430,000 in annual revenue protection from fraud and partner-program
leakage, an 85% reduction in manual reconciliation, and reporting that is three times
faster. It also reports nearly $180,000 in prevented fraudulent redemptions. These are
claims in the supplied case-study document.

Technologies named include Databricks, Unity Catalog, Delta Live Tables, MLflow, Azure
Data Factory, Power BI, point-of-sale integrations, mobile analytics, partner APIs, and
fraud-detection models. The filename labels the case study “Racetrac,” while the document
body describes the client generically; answers should make that attribution distinction
clear.
