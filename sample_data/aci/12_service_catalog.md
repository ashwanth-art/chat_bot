# ACI Infotech services

ACI presents eleven enterprise technology practice areas:

1. **Data Engineering** - Databricks lakehouses, Snowflake warehouses, governed data,
   lineage, data quality, and real-time pipelines for analytics and AI.
2. **Applied AI and ML** - production GenAI assistants, RAG and agent systems,
   forecasting, recommendations, MLOps, evaluation, guardrails, and model monitoring.
3. **Cloud Modernization** - landing zones, migrations, parallel cutovers, FinOps, and
   Kubernetes platforms designed to reduce cost without disrupting operations.
4. **MarTech and CDP** - customer-data platforms, customer 360, journey orchestration,
   marketing analytics, segmentation, and measurable activation.
5. **Digital Transformation** - ERP and process modernization, ServiceNow workflows,
   robotic process automation, document AI, and enterprise integration.
6. **Application Development** - production-grade web, enterprise, API, and AI-powered
   applications integrated with existing data and AI platforms.
7. **Quality Engineering** - in-sprint automation, AI-assisted test generation and
   maintenance, CI/CD quality gates, performance testing, security testing, and
   production telemetry.
8. **Cyber Security** - zero-trust architecture, identity, DevSecOps, cloud-security
   posture, vulnerability management, compliance, and managed security operations.
9. **Advisory and Strategy** - buildable technology roadmaps, architecture, platform
   choices, governance, operating models, and cost plans.
10. **GCC and Captive Operations** - engineering and operations centers in India and
    Latin America with documented governance and transfer paths.
11. **Managed Operations** - 24/7 NOC and SOC, infrastructure and application support,
    observability, SRE, incident response, and L1-to-L3 escalation under SLAs.

ACI says these practices use a common production-engineering standard. Its platform
ecosystem includes Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, SAP,
ServiceNow, Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Braze, OpenAI, and related enterprise
tools. A platform is selected according to the workload rather than presented as a
mandatory component of every engagement.
