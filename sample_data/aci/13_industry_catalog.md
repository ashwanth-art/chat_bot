# Industries served by ACI Infotech

ACI's official industry catalog lists eight primary sectors:

1. **Financial Services** - governed data platforms, real-time fraud and risk models,
   audit-ready lineage, core modernization, personalization, and regulated operations.
2. **Retail and Consumer** - unified customer and inventory data, loyalty intelligence,
   customer-data platforms, demand forecasting, personalization, and omnichannel
   analytics.
3. **Healthcare and Life Sciences** - FHIR and HL7 integration, patient and claims data,
   population-health analytics, research platforms, compliant cloud environments, and
   governed clinical AI.
4. **Hospitality and Food Services** - unified guest, procurement, menu, retail, and
   operational data across properties and regions, with real-time analytics.
5. **Manufacturing** - industrial IoT analytics, supply-chain integration, predictive
   maintenance, quality systems, ERP modernization, and plant operations.
6. **Energy and Utilities** - grid and asset analytics, NERC CIP-oriented controls,
   operational-data integration, forecasting, field operations, and managed security.
7. **Oil and Gas** - integration of SCADA, historians, field systems, and ERP data into
   governed platforms supporting operations, maintenance, safety, and reporting.
8. **Transportation and Logistics** - route optimization, fleet and shipment visibility,
   order-to-invoice modernization, predictive maintenance, and on-time-in-full
   performance.

Across these industries, ACI emphasizes production-grade data, AI, cloud, security, and
managed operations for regulated or high-volume enterprises.
